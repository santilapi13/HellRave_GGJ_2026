## What is HellRave?
It's a videogame developed in Unity Engine in 48 hours for the Global Game Jam 2026. Topic of this year: Mask

A hellish party filled with demons, music, and chaos… and infiltrated humans.

In this game, you play as a human who sneaked into a demon party by wearing a mask to blend in. Hide in the crowd, watch your surroundings, and eliminate the other players without raising suspicion.

But beware: everyone else is a human in disguise too, and only one will survive unnoticed.

🎭 Deception, stealth, and betrayal in a party where no one is what they seem.

👥 Local multiplayer for 2 to 4 players.

🏆 Be the last hidden human standing.
